Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
[the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441](https://make.fun/competitions/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441):
a program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to choose and
to act, specifically the agency that passes between two agents when they agree
on one plan.

## The poem

> You brought the yes. I brought the no.
> We laid them down, side by side,
> and between them found a third word —
> ours.
> Not mine to give, not yours to take:
> a door neither hand opens alone.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
